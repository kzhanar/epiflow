# Methodology source register

**Purpose:** identify authoritative sources that EpiFlow methodology and safeguards should be mapped against. This is a source register, not epidemiologic guidance, a protocol, or evidence that EpiFlow is validated or approved for operational use. It does not select methods or resolve conflicts among sources.

Before using a source to support a method or claim, consult the original publication, verify its current version and applicability to the jurisdiction, population, setting, and question, and record the access date and relevant section. Do not rely on AI-generated summaries or citations without checking them against the source. Apply the relevant public-health authority's current guidance and applicable law or institutional policy.

## Mapping and maintenance

For each proposed methodology or safeguard, record the source's exact title, issuing body, publication or update date and version, stable link or identifier, access date, and the relevant page, section, or recommendation. Explain which EpiFlow component it informs, whether the source applies to the intended jurisdiction and use, and any limitations, conflicts, or superseding guidance. Have qualified reviewers assess substantive mappings; a citation alone does not establish that a method is appropriate.

Review this register when EpiFlow's intended uses change and periodically for new or superseding primary guidance. Add sources only after verifying their authenticity and relevance. Where sources differ or applicability is uncertain, document the issue for qualified human and competent-authority review rather than resolving it by inference.
